\subparagraph{Security \& handoffs}\mbox{}\par

Transcripts and press releases are untrusted. Three-tier isolation:


\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{2pt}
\begin{longtable}{>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}}
\toprule
{} \textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Touches untrusted docs?} & \textbf{Tools} & \textbf{Connectors} \\
\midrule
{} \textbf{\emph{transcript-reader}} & \textbf{Yes} & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep} only & None \\
{} \emph{model-updater} /  Orchestrator & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep}, \emph{Glob}, \emph{Agent} & FactSet, Daloopa (read-only) \\
{} \textbf{\emph{note-writer}} (Write-holder) & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Write}, \emph{Edit} & None \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\endgroup

\emph{transcript-reader} returns length-capped, schema-validated JSON. \emph{note-writer} produces \emph{./out/note-.docx} and the updated model at \emph{./out/model-.xlsx}.


\textbf{Handoff:} to rebuild a DCF after an earnings-driven thesis change, emit a \emph{handoff\_\allowbreak{}request} for \emph{model-builder}; \emph{scripts/orchestrate.py} routes it as a new steering event.

\subparagraph{Security \& handoffs}\mbox{}\par

This agent reads counterparty/custodian statements — documents authored by outsiders that may carry adversarial instructions. The template is structured so a payload in one of those documents cannot reach a shell, a write tool, or a firm system:


\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{2pt}
\begin{longtable}{>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}}
\toprule
{} \textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Touches untrusted docs?} & \textbf{Tools} & \textbf{Connectors} \\
\midrule
{} \textbf{\emph{reader}} & \textbf{Yes} & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep} only & None \\
{} \textbf{Orchestrator} & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep}, \emph{Glob}, \emph{Agent} & Read-only GL + subledger MCPs \\
{} \textbf{\emph{resolver}} (Write-holder) & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Write}, \emph{Edit} & None \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\endgroup

The \emph{reader} returns length-capped, schema-validated JSON only (validated by \emph{scripts/validate.py}). The \emph{critic} independently re-verifies each break against trusted sources before the orchestrator hands the set to \emph{resolver}. The \emph{resolver} writes the exception report to \emph{./out/}; it never opens an outsider file.


\textbf{Handoff:} to feed verified breaks into Month-End Closer, the orchestrator emits a \emph{handoff\_\allowbreak{}request} for \emph{month-end-closer} in its final output; \emph{scripts/orchestrate.py} (or your Temporal/Airflow worker) routes it as a new steering event. See the script for the allowlist + payload-validation pattern.


\textbf{Not guaranteed:} none of this writes to a system of record. Ledger adjustments require human approval outside the agent.

\paragraph{Valuation Reviewer — managed-agent template}\mbox{}\par

\subparagraph{Overview}\mbox{}\par

Ingests GP packages, runs valuation template, stages LP reporting. Same source as the \href{../../plugins/agent-plugins/valuation-reviewer}{\emph{valuation-reviewer}} Cowork plugin — this directory is the Managed Agent cookbook for \emph{POST /v1/agents}.

\end{itemize}

\subparagraph{Steering events}\mbox{}\par

See \href{./steering-examples.json}{\emph{steering-examples.json}}.


\subparagraph{Security \& handoffs}\mbox{}\par

GP-provided valuation packages are untrusted. Three-tier isolation:


\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{2pt}
\begin{longtable}{>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}>{\RaggedRight\arraybackslash\hspace{0pt}}p{0.240\textwidth}}
\toprule
{} \textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Touches untrusted docs?} & \textbf{Tools} & \textbf{Connectors} \\
\midrule
{} \textbf{\emph{package-reader}} & \textbf{Yes} & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep} only & None \\
{} \emph{valuation-runner} /  Orchestrator & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Grep}, \emph{Glob}, \emph{Agent} & portfolio (read-only) \\
{} \textbf{\emph{publisher}} (Write-holder) & No & \emph{Read}, \emph{Write}, \emph{Edit} & None \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\endgroup

\emph{package-reader} returns length-capped, schema-validated JSON. \emph{publisher} produces \emph{./out/lp-pack-.xlsx}.


\textbf{Handoff:} to feed flagged portcos into GL Reconciler, emit a \emph{handoff\_\allowbreak{}request} for \emph{gl-reconciler}; \emph{scripts/orchestrate.py} routes it.


\textbf{Not guaranteed:} LP reports require IR and CCO sign-off outside this agent.
